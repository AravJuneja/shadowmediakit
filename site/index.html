Synopsis

Two friends face off in a video game with sleek, realistic graphics, but their friendly competition quickly turns into something much more intense. As tension builds and emotions take over, the line between the game and reality begins to blur, leading to explosive consequences. One friend may win the game, but he soon discovers that victory in the virtual world can come at a very real cost.

Director's Statement

As the artist, director, director of photography, and producer of this project, I shaped the music video from both the musical and visual sides. I put months of preparation into the project, and I'm very proud of the final result and what we were able to create.

The song is meant to represent controlled chaos, and I wanted the film to reflect that through the fighting, conflicts, lighting, and camera movement throughout the story. One thing I am obsessed with as a filmmaker is silhouettes because of the symbolism and storytelling they can create. I wanted to use silhouettes not only as a visual style, but also as another way of telling the story through lighting.

For the cinematography, I chose fast-tracking shots to emphasize how chaotic the fighting feels while still maintaining a sense of control behind the camera. I also incorporated controlled rolling movements to push that feeling further and make the camera itself feel like part of the action.

One of my biggest inspirations while brainstorming the video was a mobile game I used to play during lunch in high school called Shadow Fighter. I always had a lot of fun playing it and was drawn to its visual style, especially its use of silhouettes and fighting. That inspiration became an important part of the look, movement, and energy I wanted to bring to this project.
